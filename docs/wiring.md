# Wiring

## Per Pack
- Class‑T fuse close to pack
- Pre-charge path: relay + **100–220 Ω, 50–100 W** resistor
- Main contactor: EV200/Gigavac class, 24 V coil
- Orion BMS: “Enable/Charger Safety” **in series** with coil supply
- Equal-length, equal-gauge cables to the DC bus

## Bus
- Common +/− busbars (or Lynx). Keep thermal margin; size for current.
- Quattro legs fused per Victron manual.
